This is the full PokeSurvivor Pokédex: all 966 Pokémon you can play as a partner, from all 9 regions. Each card shows the sprite, National Pokédex number, name and types. 29 are marked Starter (you can pick them on your first run) and 87 are marked Legendary. Everything else comes from the gacha. Use the search box or the type filter to find a Pokémon fast.

## How to get Pokémon in PokeSurvivor

- **Starters (29).** The 27 regional starters plus Psyduck and Slowpoke. A personality quiz suggests one, or you choose another. See our [best starter guide](/best-pokemon/) and the [tier list](/tier-list/).
- **Gacha (everything else).** Each region has its own gacha. Tickets drop while you play and are never sold for real money. Bosses and rift legendaries drop ×10 tickets, so [rifts](/rifts/) are the fastest source.
- **Shinies.** About 1 in 100 gacha pulls is shiny, and wild shinies are 1 in 4,096. We have not confirmed whether shinies have different stats.
- **Upgrades.** Permanent upgrades are bought with coins per Pokémon, so it pays to pick one partner and invest in it.
