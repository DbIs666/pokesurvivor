The best starter in PokeSurvivor for most new players is Mudkip or Sobble, both Water Gun users. The strongest starter on paper is Scorbunny (attack multiplier 1.09), and the best pick across a full run in our [tier list](/tier-list/) is Froakie, the fastest starter. This page covers one decision: which Pokémon to take on your first runs, roughly the first 30 minutes of play. For rankings across early, mid and late game, see the [PokeSurvivor tier list](/tier-list/).

## How the first pick works

New players take a short personality quiz ("Your personality is…"). It suggests a partner, and you can accept or choose another. The picker describes the pool as "the starters from every region, plus two more friends": the 27 regional starters from Kanto to Paldea, plus Psyduck and Slowpoke. That's 29 options.

The quiz result is only a suggestion. Pick based on the starting move, since it's the one attack you'll have from the first second.

## Best Starter Pokémon for Beginners: Our 3 Picks

These are our recommendations, based on client data and the moves list rather than a community consensus (none exists yet).

1. **Mudkip or Sobble (Water Gun).** Reliable range and pierce, and among the highest attack multipliers (1.08). Sobble is also fast (107). Scald needs the Power card, which is easy to want anyway.
2. **Bulbasaur, Chikorita, Turtwig or Grookey (Vine Whip).** A wide arc that clears crowds around you. Power Whip (×1.9 damage, 360°) also evolves with Power. Chespin also has Vine Whip. Of these, Grookey is the fastest (105) and Turtwig hits hardest (1.07).
3. **Scorbunny (Ember).** The highest attack multiplier of any starter (1.09). Ember fires one shot at a time early, but Fire Blast is a big jump.

If you want something different:

- **Fast:** Froakie has the top speed (108) and Water Gun. Speed is your main defence when you only move.
- **Tanky:** Fuecoco has 100 HP with Ember, and Rowlet has 101 with Razor Leaf. Slowpoke has the most HP (110) but is the slowest starter (84), so we don't suggest it for a first run.
- **Control:** Popplio's and Piplup's Bubble slows enemies, which buys space. Piplup has more HP (94 vs 92).

Base stats between starters are close, so your first pick won't make or break a run.

## Best Pokémon in PokeSurvivor Overall (Beyond Starters)

Starters are only the first step. The rest of the roster, including legendaries, comes from the per-region gacha, and no one has published tested rankings for those yet. Among starters, our [tier list](/tier-list/) puts Mudkip and Sobble on top early, the Water Gun and Vine Whip users on top in mid game, and Froakie on top late.

What makes a Pokémon strong over a full run, in our assessment:

- **A move that evolves with a card you want anyway.** Power is the general damage card, so Scald, Fire Blast and Power Whip come online without bending your build.
- **Speed.** You only move, so faster partners dodge more and pick up XP sooner.
- **Type coverage for rifts.** Each rift arena has a type, terrain and weather. See the [rifts and legendaries guide](/rifts/) for the 18 arenas.

We'll add non-starter picks here once we have data we can stand behind.

## After the first pick

- **Gacha:** other partners come from per-region gacha. You earn tickets by playing; bosses and rift legendaries drop ×10 tickets. See the [rifts and legendaries guide](/rifts/).
- **Upgrades:** permanent upgrades bought with coins are per Pokémon. Pick one partner to invest in at first rather than spreading coins.
- **Your first run:** the [How to Play](/how-to-play/) guide covers level-up cards, evolutions and enemy types.
- **Playing with friends:** XP is shared in co-op, so any starter works. The [co-op guide](/co-op/) explains how to set up a room.
